How I like to work

The work should feel considered, not merely clever.

I care about the seams that are easy to ignore: a tool call that needs a boundary, a workflow that needs a recovery path, or a product decision that needs a human in the loop.

01 / START WITH THE REAL JOB

Build for the workflow, not the demo.

The most interesting AI experiences remove friction from work people already do, without making the system mysterious.

02 / MAKE TRUST VISIBLE

Intelligence needs clear edges.

Context, permissions, memory, and deletion should have intentional behavior. The user should not have to guess what the system knows.

03 / KEEP LEARNING IN THE BUILD

Choose problems that stretch the system.

I am early in my career and deliberately look for projects that make me learn faster than a comfortable solution would.

Cognaxis / 2026

Memory with boundaries.

Cognaxis began during the Google GenAI Academy. It is an AI-assisted journal that treats memory as a trust problem: who owns the context, what can a model see, and what should disappear when a person deletes it?
